# FiresMounts

Rideable horses, flying mounts and a leaping, gliding Mini Fader for Valheim, with levels, saddle looks and a
whistle to call your mount from anywhere.

## What's new in 0.1.149

- Updated to work with the current Fires Unified Core. No gameplay changes.

## What you get

- **Horses.** Wild herds roam the Plains, and the odd lone horse shows up in the Meadows. Every horse has its own
  coat, mane and tail.
- **Flying mounts.** Hatch a Mini Moder or a Drake from an egg, or tame a wild drake. A flyer that follows you
  fights alongside you.
- **Mini Fader.** A ground mount that leaps, glides and climbs walls.
- **Levels 1-20.** Mounts get stronger, faster and tougher as they level. Flyers can fly higher; level 10 unlocks a
  boost in the air and level 20 earns a gold name.
- **Looks.** Saddle dyes and essences change your mount's colours.
- **Mount whistle** to call your last mount from anywhere in the world.

## How to use

**Horses**
- Tame a wild horse like a Lox: feed it Carrot, Turnip, Blueberries, Raspberries, Barley or Dandelion and keep it
  calm (about 20 minutes).
- Hold a Lox saddle and press **E** on the tamed horse to saddle it (Alt+E on the saddle takes it off).
- **E** mounts and dismounts. **W** speeds up through walk, trot, canter and gallop; **Shift** goes to full gallop.
  **A / D** steer, **S** slows down, **tap C** for a steady walk. **Jump** hops obstacles; **Attack** rears up and
  strikes (never hurts players or tamed animals).

**Flying mounts**
- Build a **Mount nest** (Stone 20, Crystal 5, Dragon tears 2) and put an egg in it. It hatches after a day. Moder
  drops a Moder egg; mountain drakes sometimes drop a Drake egg. Wild drakes can be tamed with Freeze glands or
  Crystal once Bonemass is defeated.
- Craft a **Sky saddle** at the Artisan table (Dragon tears 5, Silver 10, Deer hide 10, Linen thread 10). Without a
  saddle, E switches follow / stay.
- Flying: **Shift** speeds up and takes off, **W / S** forward and slow down, **A / D** bank, **Q / E** turn. Hold
  **Space** to climb (double-tap to hover), hold **Ctrl** to descend and land. Double-tap **E** to jump off in mid-air.
- Left / middle mouse use the mount's attacks (Alt + mouse wheel picks the special). Right mouse raises a bubble
  shield on you and your mount.
- Flying uses stamina - a tired flyer sinks and lands.

**Mini Fader**
- The Fader drops a Fader egg; hatch it in a Mount nest and saddle it with a Sky saddle.
- Hold **Space** to charge a big leap (hold A or D for a side leap), then glide. Off a cliff, hold Space to glide.
  No fall damage while riding.
- Ride into a steep cliff or wall to climb it: W / S up and down, A / D along it, Space jumps off.

**Levels and looks**
- XP comes from riding, flying, kills and feeding. At 0 stamina a mount is Tired until it rests on the ground.
- Craft a **Saddle dye** at the workbench and use it on a saddled mount (three looks per mount).
- Use an **essence** (brewed at the Cauldron) on a tamed mount or an egg to give it that essence's colours. An
  Essence of Clarity resets it.

**Mount whistle**
- Craft it at the workbench (Wood 2, Leather scraps 2) and carry it. **Double-tap H** and the last mount you rode
  appears behind you. 30 seconds between calls.

## Install

Install on every player **and** on the dedicated server. The server's gameplay settings apply to everyone; looks
and sound settings are per player.

## Requires

- BepInExPack for Valheim
- FiresUnifiedCore

## Good to know

- Settings are in the Fires config window (F1). The whistle key can be changed there, and so can the flying keys
  (turn left and right, abilities, stop) and the flight preset.
- If you die while riding a flyer, it waits at your tombstone.
- The in-game "? Help" panel (pause menu) has a FiresMounts section with every control.
